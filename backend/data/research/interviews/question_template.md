# User Interview Protocol: Wishlist to Purchase Behavior Guide

> **Purpose:** Reference guide for qualitative user interview analysis across all 10 Research Questions (RQ1–RQ10).
> **Location:** `backend/data/research/interviews/question_template.md`

---

## 1. Warm Up Questions
1. Tell me a bit about how you usually shop for clothes — online or offline, planned or impulsive?
2. When's the last time you bought something fashion-related? Which App?

## 2. Wishlist Adding Behavior
1. Think of the most recent thing you added to your wishlist. Where were you, what were you doing, and what made you hit "save" instead of "buy now"?
2. What uncertainties remain after you have identified a product you like?

## 3. Wishlist to Cart Behavior
1. Think about the last time you actually bought something that had been sitting in your wishlist. Walk me through that purchase, what made that purchase happen that day?
2. How long had it been sitting there before you bought it?
3. Did you go looking for it or did something bring it back to your attention? (notification, social media, etc.)

## 4. Reason for Adding But Not Buying
1. Now think of something you wishlisted but never bought, and either forgot about or deleted. Tell me that story.
2. Was there a moment you consciously gave up on it or did it just fade out? What was going on around that time?
3. What's sitting in your wishlist right now that you'll probably never buy? Why is it still there?

## 5. What is the Wishlist for? (Mental Model)
1. If you had to describe what your wishlist actually is to you (a cart in waiting, a dream board, a way to not lose track of things, something else — how would you put it?)
2. Do you ever use it for things other than eventually buying for yourself? (gifting, comparing options, styling ideas, etc.)

## 6. Decision Factors
1. If that item's price never dropped, is there anything else that would make you buy it?
2. Has the sheer number of items in your wishlist ever made it harder to decide or made you avoid opening it altogether? Tell me about that.
3. When you're unsure about fit, quality or how something will actually look, what do you currently do to resolve that? Does it work?
4. What information do you seek outside Myntra/AJIO before purchasing?
5. How do you compare multiple shortlisted products?

## 7. Notification Engagement
1. Has a notification ever brought back interest in something you'd basically forgotten about? What happened?
2. Is there a reminder or alert you wish you got that you currently don't?

## 8. Reactions to Non-Monetary Solution Directions
1. If your wishlist could do one thing better for you, not related to price, what would it be?
   - A way to compare 2–3 wishlisted items side by side
   - The wishlist auto-sorting or decluttering itself over time
   - Seeing how other people styled or reviewed a saved item
   - A nudge after an item's been sitting for a while
2. Would that have changed anything in the stories you just told me? Why or why not?
3. Is there anything about your wishlist experience that frustrates you that we haven't touched on?
4. If you could change exactly one thing about how it works, what would it be?
